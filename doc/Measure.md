# Measure units

- specifies measurement units of the real physical world
- type system blocks your errors with adding and multiplicating kilograms, inches, pounds, watts and mm/sec

https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/fsharp/language-reference/units-of-measure

The necessity to have units of Measure in the programing language type system is determined by classical errors with the conversion of real-world units and their derivatives. There are many known cases of such errors in software, which led to significant material losses and human casualties.
